I am a **Computer Science Ph.D. researcher at Oregon State University**, advised by [Prof. Xiao Fu](https://web.engr.oregonstate.edu/~fuxia/), with a minor in Artificial Intelligence. My expected graduation is **December 2026**.

My research connects **generative modeling, statistical signal processing, and numerical optimization**. I develop methods that use pretrained diffusion and score-based models as priors for inference from noisy, incomplete, and quantized observations. Applications include image restoration, hyperspectral and single-pixel spectral reconstruction, radio-map estimation, and wireless channel recovery.

My recent work includes a convergent plug-and-play ADMM framework with score-based denoisers (**ICLR 2026**) and recoverability analysis and algorithms for MIMO channel estimation from bits (**IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing, 2025**). I also study synchronization in diffusion and flow models, designing coupled training and sampling methods for correlated generations. Earlier, I worked with Prof. Arash Termehchy on interactive learning from noisy and evolving human feedback.

Before joining OSU, I worked at **[Fusemachines](https://fusemachines.com)** from October 2018 to August 2021. I developed production advertising recommendation models and ML pipelines supporting millions of push notifications per day, contributed to document AI and Nepali speech recognition, and mentored two ML engineers. I received my bachelor's degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from [Pulchowk Campus](https://pcampus.edu.np).
